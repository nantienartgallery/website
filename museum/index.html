The Humanistic Buddhism in Oceania Museum stands as a living chronicle of how Humanistic Buddhism has been established and practiced throughout the entire Oceania region.

The museum documents the collective history, propagation journey, and societal contributions of all Fo Guang Shan temples and centers across Australia and New Zealand, featuring key highlights such as:

Regional Development & Propagation History
Chronicling the growth and footprint of Humanistic Buddhism across Oceania.
Temple Foundations & Milestones
Preserving the construction histories and key events of regional temples, including the founding of Nan Tien Temple.
Educational Networks
Highlighting global educational initiatives through the Fo Guang Shan University Consortium and the higher education curriculum of Nan Tien Institute.
Culture, Art, and Education
Demonstrating how temples throughout Australia and New Zealand actively serve local communities through cultural engagement, artistic promotion, and education.

Welcome to the Humanistic Buddhism in Oceania Museum

This museum witnesses the history and active propagation of Humanistic Buddhism across Oceania. Encompassing the collective history of all Fo Guang Shan temples and centers throughout Australia and New Zealand, the exhibits showcase regional construction milestones, historical chronologies, and educational initiatives like the Fo Guang Shan University Consortium and Nan Tien Institute—highlighting a shared commitment to serving local society through culture, art, and education.
