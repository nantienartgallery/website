Acknowledgement

We acknowledge the Dharawal people, the Traditional Custodians of the land on which the gallery stands, and pay our respects to Elders past and present.

We pay homage to the Triple Gem — the Buddha, the Dharma and the Sangha — and pay tribute to Venerable Master Hsing Yun, Founder of Fo Guang Shan, whose vision of Humanistic Buddhism inspired this gallery.
