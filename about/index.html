Vision & mission

At Nan Tien Art Gallery, we are dedicated to enriching minds and promoting Humanistic Buddhism through the transformative power of culture and art.

Our gallery serves as an open platform for creators, encouraging meaningful cultural dialogue and creative exchange. Through curated exhibitions and community education, we aim to elevate daily aesthetics, nourish spiritual growth, and foster overall health and well-being. By preserving a meaningful permanent collection, we strive to inspire creativity, drive social development, and pass on cultural wisdom for generations to come.

Our core pillars

Nan Tien Art Gallery invites visitors into a space where artistic expression meets cultural heritage and spiritual enrichment. Our mission is guided by four core pillars:

01

Spiritual & Cultural Enrichment

Utilizing art and culture to promote Humanistic Buddhism and foster inner peace.

02

Artistic Platform & Exchange

Providing artists with a collaborative space to display work and bridge cultural divides.

03

Aesthetics & Well-Being

Offering exhibitions and educational outreach designed to enhance everyday life aesthetics and holistic well-being.

04

Heritage & Innovation

Curating collections that ignite creative thinking, support community growth, and preserve cultural knowledge.

Acknowledgement

The Nan Tien Art Gallery acknowledges the Dharawal people, the Traditional Custodians of the land on which the gallery stands, and pay our respects to Elders past and present. We recognise their continuing connection to land, sea, and community.

The Gallery also respectfully pay homage to the Triple Gems — the Buddha, the Dharma and the Sangha and pays tribute to Venerable Master Hsing Yun, the Founding Master of Fo Guang Shan, whose vision of Humanistic Buddhism has inspired the establishment and development of Buddhist education, culture and arts around the world, including Nan Tien Temple, Nan Tien Institute and Nan Tien Art Gallery.
